Commerce principles

Education stays independent.

  • A product does not receive a higher evidence rating because AutonoHealth sells it.
  • Commercial relationships and affiliate links are disclosed clearly.
  • No purchase is framed as medically necessary.
  • Active ingredients, intended purpose, limitations, and major safety notes are easy to find.
More than kits

Room to expand thoughtfully.

The Shop can eventually include home and travel preparedness, monitoring accessories, wellness tools, journals, educational materials, and carefully selected third-party products.

Product curation and medical content use separate review workflows, even when they appear on the same platform.
