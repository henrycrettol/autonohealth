Evidence without the fog

One claim. One outcome. One honest rating.

“Does it work?” is rarely specific enough. AutonoHealth rates evidence for a defined treatment, use, population, and outcome—then explains the limitations in plain language.

How ratings work
AStrong

Consistent support from high-quality evidence or authoritative guidance.

BModerate

Supportive findings with limitations that reduce certainty.

CLimited or mixed

Early, small, inconsistent, indirect, or context-dependent research.

DInsufficient

Not enough reliable evidence to estimate benefit confidently.
