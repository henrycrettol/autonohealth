About AutonoHealth

Health information should help people feel capable — not overwhelmed.

AutonoHealth is an evidence-based education and self-management platform designed to make health conditions, treatment options, daily habits, and informed conversations easier to navigate.

Our mission

Make trustworthy health information understandable and useful.

AutonoHealth helps people understand conditions, compare lifestyle, natural-product, and medication options, track daily health behaviors, and build sustainable habits—without pretending that education is diagnosis.

Meet Otto

A friendly guide with clear boundaries.

Otto helps users navigate, explain evidence, review permitted tracking patterns, celebrate progress, and prepare next questions. Otto does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace professional care.

What guides the platform

Evidence first. People centered. Honest about limits.

Transparent evidence

Claims reflect the quality, consistency, relevance, and limitations of current research.

Support, not replacement

The platform improves understanding and professional conversations without practicing medicine.

User control

People choose what to track, what to share, and what information Otto may use.

Privacy by design

Health data deserves deliberate protection.

When signed out, tracker entries stay in this browser. When you sign in, Firebase Authentication identifies your account and tracker entries are stored in your private Firestore path so they can sync across devices. AutonoHealth does not keep a second copy of your Google profile.

Questions submitted to Otto are processed by OpenAI through an AutonoHealth server. Tracker entries are not included unless you explicitly enable the optional 14-day summary; the chat lets you preview exactly what will be shared and turn sharing off. Use “Delete all tracker data” on the tracker page to remove synced entries and the tracker template. Firebase Authentication account information is managed separately by Google/Firebase.

Editorial independence

Commerce does not rewrite the evidence.

Shop content, sponsorships, and affiliate relationships should be disclosed clearly and kept separate from evidence ratings, editorial conclusions, and Otto’s educational guidance.
